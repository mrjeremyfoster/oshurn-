# Oshurn Financial — Lean Solo Boutique Launch Plan

## 1. Business model

Oshurn Financial is intentionally launching as a **solo, owner-operated boutique financial-services practice**. The public experience should look polished and substantial without creating unnecessary staffing, software, or overhead.

Initial objective:

> Get licensed → establish carrier/BGA infrastructure → buy a small number of qualified leads → sell and service the business personally → measure results → reinvest profitable cash flow → improve.

No agent recruiting, large CRM build, or nationwide expansion is required at launch.

## 2. Operating structure — planned, subject to professional review

Intended privacy/governance model:

- Owner/member: user retains ownership/economic interest.
- Corporate Creations: proposed nominee-manager provider, only if a Texas attorney confirms the structure is lawful, accurately disclosed, and appropriate for banking, tax, insurance licensing, beneficial-ownership, and governance requirements.
- Registered agent: professional service for service of process and official notices.
- Austin virtual office: professional business presence where appropriate and accepted by relevant regulators/bank/providers.
- Oshurn Financial: public-facing business brand.

Principle:

> **Maximum lawful privacy. Maximum professional transparency where required.**

Do not use a nominee/manager arrangement to conceal required ownership, authority, licensing, tax, banking, or regulatory information.

## 3. Licensing

Insurance production requires the appropriate license and applicable appointments/authorization.

Launch gate:

**Required license verified → BGA/carrier contracting → appointments/authorization → production.**

Oshurn may provide informational guidance about the licensing process but will not represent itself as replacing state-required education, exams, fingerprints, applications, or other licensing requirements.

## 4. BGA / carrier infrastructure

Primary BGA/FMO candidate:

**Brokers Alliance**

Intended functions to verify during onboarding:

- Carrier contracting
- Carrier appointments
- Licensing/contracting support
- Product and case support
- Underwriting/case management resources
- Quoting/case-design technology
- Agent technology resources
- BGA training and carrier support

Before committing, confirm the specific Texas product lines/carriers desired and confirm that independently sourced leads may be used.

## 5. Initial lead source

Primary test source:

**BuyLeads**

Initial budget:

> **$210/month**

The working assumption is approximately five $42 life-insurance leads per month, subject to current pricing, availability, market, product, and vendor terms.

The purpose of the initial budget is experimentation, not volume.

### Lead compliance documentation — required before purchase/use

For every purchased lead source, request and retain documentation sufficient to establish:

- Lead-generation source
- Lead form/landing page URL
- Exact consent language shown to the consumer
- Date/time of submission
- Phone number submitted
- Available consent record/metadata
- Whether the lead is exclusive or shared
- Lead delivery timestamp
- Vendor's description of applicable TCPA/DNC compliance processes
- Opt-out handling process
- Replacement/refund policy

**SMS/OTP verification is not treated as proof of all required telemarketing consent.**

Before using a purchased lead, confirm the vendor can provide an appropriate consent record and that Oshurn's calling/texting practices comply with applicable federal and state law, carrier rules, and insurance requirements.

## 6. Lead-to-sale workflow

**Lead received**
→ contact promptly
→ discovery/revelation question
→ understand protection need
→ explore family/financial objectives
→ establish realistic budget
→ explain application/underwriting
→ recommend appropriate options within authority
→ complete application accurately
→ submit through BGA/carrier
→ satisfy underwriting requirements
→ carrier decision
→ policy delivery/placement when applicable
→ ongoing client service

## 7. Sales measurement

Track six core production numbers:

- Leads purchased
- Contacts/conversations
- Appointments
- Applications
- Approved/issued/placed business
- Commission/revenue

Also calculate:

- Lead cost
- Contact rate
- Appointment rate
- Application rate
- Placement rate
- Cost per appointment
- Cost per placed policy
- Commission per lead
- Net profit after lead expense
- Persistency/chargeback experience over time

The objective is to improve **conversion quality**, not simply purchase more leads.

## 8. Reinvestment strategy

Start with the fixed test budget.

**$210 → leads → sales → commission → evaluate net profit → reinvest a disciplined portion into additional leads.**

Do not increase lead spending solely because revenue increased. Increase it when conversion, underwriting, placement, and cash-flow results support the decision.

## 9. Business infrastructure

### Banking

Preferred starting bank:

**Bank of America — Business Advantage Fundamentals Banking**

Use a dedicated business account and keep business and personal funds separate.

Confirm current fees, waiver requirements, eligibility, and required formation documents before opening.

### Bookkeeping

Preferred low-overhead bookkeeping platform:

**Wave**

Use it for basic transaction categorization, bookkeeping, invoicing where needed, and financial reporting. Have a CPA/tax professional confirm the appropriate tax and recordkeeping setup.

### Professional support

Maintain access to:

- CPA/tax professional
- Business/entity attorney
- Insurance/compliance resources
- BGA/case-management support

## 10. Website

The Oshurn website is the public-facing front door.

Design standard:

- Premium white-space aesthetic
- Professional and trustworthy
- Clear service explanations
- Simple consultation scheduling
- Clear contact options
- Business identity rather than personal-life branding
- Required disclosures/privacy information as applicable

The website should generate trust and consultations, not try to be a giant software platform at launch.

## 11. Free/low-cost marketing engine

Oshurn will build an owned organic marketing system alongside paid leads.

Initial channels:

- Facebook
- Instagram
- LinkedIn
- Google Business Profile where appropriate
- Educational website content
- Referral relationships
- Community/networking opportunities
- Short-form educational video when practical

The long-term goal is to gradually reduce dependence on purchased leads by generating more **owned, organic, referral, and eventually directly acquired paid traffic** through Oshurn's own funnel.

## 12. Social posting system

Use a low-overhead scheduler such as Buffer's current free tier if it remains suitable.

Initial cadence:

**3 useful posts per week** rather than high-volume posting.

Content pillars:

### Education

- Term vs. permanent coverage
- What underwriting means
- Why beneficiaries matter
- How life insurance works
- Common insurance misconceptions
- What to know before applying
- Protection planning basics

### Trust

- Oshurn philosophy
- Client-service principles
- Licensing verification education
- What happens during the application process
- How underwriting works
- Professional explanations of common questions

### Engagement

- Questions clients commonly ask
- Short financial/protection tips
- Myth vs. fact
- Simple scenarios
- Polls/questions where compliant and appropriate

### Conversion

Use occasional clear calls to action:

> **Need help reviewing your protection needs? Schedule a consultation with Oshurn Financial.**

All advertising and social content must follow applicable insurance, advertising, disclosure, privacy, TCPA/telemarketing, and carrier/BGA rules.

## 13. Monthly operating rhythm

### Week 1

- Review prior month's lead metrics
- Purchase the planned lead volume
- Schedule social content
- Review pending cases
- Review bookkeeping

### Week 2

- Work every assigned lead promptly
- Conduct consultations
- Submit applications
- Follow up on underwriting requirements

### Week 3

- Client follow-up
- Educational content
- Review lead quality
- Review appointment and application conversion

### Week 4

- Close outstanding cases
- Reconcile commissions/expenses
- Update Wave
- Calculate net profit
- Decide whether next month's lead budget stays at $210 or changes based on actual results

## 14. Future owned lead-generation engine

When the sales process is proven, build Oshurn's own lead funnel:

**Search/social/referrals/organic content**
→ Oshurn educational page
→ protection-review offer
→ compliant intake form
→ consent capture
→ lead record
→ prompt contact
→ consultation
→ application
→ underwriting
→ placement

The goal is eventually to know Oshurn's own **cost per qualified opportunity** and compare it with purchased-lead economics.

Do not launch paid advertising at scale until the offer, conversion process, consent language, tracking, disclosures, and compliance process have been reviewed.

## 15. Technology philosophy

### Launch now

Keep technology simple:

- Website
- Scheduling/intake
- Spreadsheet or lightweight lead tracker
- BGA-provided technology
- Wave
- Bank of America online banking
- Social scheduler

### Future

Only build custom CRM/client-portal/AI infrastructure after real operating volume demonstrates a need.

The previously documented CRM/performance architecture remains a future roadmap, not a launch requirement.

## 16. Privacy and professional identity

Oshurn should absorb routine business exposure rather than unnecessarily exposing personal information.

Use:

- Oshurn business email
- Oshurn business phone
- Business mailing/address solution where appropriate
- Business social profiles
- Professional client communications

Be transparent where law, licensing, carrier, banking, tax, or regulatory requirements require individual or ownership information.

## 17. Launch budget philosophy

Keep recurring overhead intentionally low.

Initial recurring lead experiment:

**$210/month**

Other costs should be evaluated individually and kept lean where possible, including:

- LLC/entity filing
- Registered agent
- Approved privacy/governance services
- Virtual office if appropriate
- Licensing
- E&O/business insurance
- Banking
- Bookkeeping
- BGA/carrier requirements
- Website/domain/software

Do not purchase expensive software simply because it is available.

## 18. Definition of success

The first milestone is not scale.

It is:

> **Prove that a small, disciplined lead budget can produce profitable placed business while providing an excellent client experience.**

Then improve:

**Better conversations → better conversion → better placement → better persistency → more profit → disciplined reinvestment.**
